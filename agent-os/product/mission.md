# Product Mission

## Problem

Personal budgeting is chaotic without a central tool: income comes from multiple sources and currencies, spending categories blur together, and it's nearly impossible to know at a glance whether you're on track with savings or blowing your budget in a specific area.

## Target Users

Built for personal use — a single user who wants full control and clarity over their own finances, including income in multiple currencies, allocation-based budgeting, and month-by-month tracking.

## Solution

MyWallet is a personal finance dashboard that structures money management around allocation percentages. You define how your income should be split across categories (food, transport, savings, etc.), log income and expenses, and the dashboard instantly shows budget vs. actual per category — with currency conversion handled automatically via manually entered monthly exchange rates.
